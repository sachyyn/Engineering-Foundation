# Confirmed decisions and research

Evidence reviewed 2026-09-25. Decisions were discussed with the tech lead, who accepted the proposal, delegated remaining recommendations, and explicitly approved implementation after requesting the updated skill's CI and independent-review requirements. This document records that approval, not a new self-approval.

## Confirmed requirements

One shared ERP for approximately 50 companies and 500 users. Each client account belongs to exactly one company; platform accounts have no client membership. Takewise administrators manage companies/accounts, assign invoice prefixes and invite the first client administrator. They have no routine access to company business records and cannot impersonate clients. Client administrators have all company permissions; staff may manage customers, invoices and inventory, including adjustments, but cannot manage users or void invoices.

Invite-only email/password authentication and password reset; MFA deferred. Initial modules are customers, recorded invoices and inventory. India, INR, whole units, one warehouse per company. Issue reduces stock, insufficient stock blocks issue, void returns stock exactly once. Correct issued invoices by void and replacement. Takewise assigns a company prefix plus running number. No payment integration or GST calculation now. Archived master records remain referentially valid; issued invoices retain snapshots. Online only, four developers on macOS/Linux using Cursor and Claude Code; tech lead reviews.

Hosting, peak concurrency, retention/export/deletion obligations, email provider, availability targets, RPO/RTO and budget remain unknown. These block production activation, not implementing the documented boundaries. Do not invent legal compliance or promise tax-ready invoices.
